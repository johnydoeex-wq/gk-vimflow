Frequently Asked Questions

Does the extension store my password?

Yes. Passwords are stored locally within your browser for autofill functionality.

Does the developer receive my password?

No. Passwords are never transmitted to the developer.

How is CAPTCHA solved?

The extension performs OCR locally using an embedded OCR engine.

Does the extension read SMS?

No. SMS messages are not accessed by the extension.

How is OTP entered?

Users may optionally configure third-party Android automation software such as MacroDroid.
